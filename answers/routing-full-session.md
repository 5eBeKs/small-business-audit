## Things to check with your accountant
- I assumed prices include VAT at 19% on both goods and shipping. Every order in the file matches that rate exactly.
- This export only shows what customers paid. It doesn't include Shopify or PayPal fees, cost of goods, or payout timing. Those come from your payout reports.
- I didn't check whether any orders fall under OSS/cross-border VAT rules. The file doesn't list customer countries, so it's worth asking about that if you ship outside Germany.
