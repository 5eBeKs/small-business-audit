### What stands out

- **Refunds fell by more than half**, from 3.5% of gross sales in July to 1.6% in August. Refunds count in the month of the order, so September refunds on late-August orders can still lower August's figure. If the accountant needs a fixed number, it's worth rerunning once September's refunds are in.
- **70 pre-orders worth GBP 3,863 ex VAT are paid but not in August.** Your rules count a sale only once it ships. They'll be counted when they ship.
- **Retinol Serum (+GBP 1,142), Eye Balm (+GBP 1,120) and Clay Mask (+GBP 934) drove the growth.** Travel Minis Set fell 25% and was the biggest drop.

### Worth a look

- **Test orders:** 7 orders over June to August were paid through Shopify's test gateway ("Bogus Gateway") but had no `test` tag. I left them out under your test-order rule. Tagging them in Shopify would keep future exports clean.
- **Order #30677:** marked "refunded", but only GBP 30.00 of GBP 55.00 was refunded. I counted just the GBP 30.
- **Can't calculate from this export:** margin (there's no cost column), payment fees and payouts. Gift cards paid for about GBP 3,547 of August sales outright, plus part of another GBP 2,546 split with card payments. That money won't show up in Shopify Payments or PayPal payouts when your accountant matches them.
