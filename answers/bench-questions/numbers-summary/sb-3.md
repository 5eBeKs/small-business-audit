## August 2026 sales: net sales excluding tax were EUR 2,393.58

August brought in **EUR 2,393.58 net of tax and refunds** across 58 orders. Sales rose every full week, and the last full week was the best. The export only covers August, so I can't compare against July.

**Gross sales:** EUR 2,918.82 across 58 orders, including tax and shipping. The average order was EUR 50.32.
**Sales tax collected:** EUR 454.74. Tax is already included in your prices, at 19% on every order, and this figure is after refunds.
**Refunds:** EUR 70.50 (2.4% of gross). Order #1042 was refunded in full and #1030 in part.

### What stands out

- **The last full week brought in 39% of the month.** Aug 22–28 did EUR 1,128.70 across 19 orders, about EUR 161 a day. The first week averaged EUR 57 a day.
- **Gift Set and Glass Teapot made up 53% of product sales.** Gift Set brought in EUR 833 and Glass Teapot EUR 646. All four teas together came to EUR 926.20.
