## August 2026 Shopify sales

August brought in **EUR 2,848.32 after refunds** from 58 orders. Sales picked up through the month, and the last full week (Aug 22–28) was the best.

**Net collected, incl. VAT:** EUR 2,848.32
**Net sales excl. VAT:** EUR 2,393.58 (net VAT at 19% is EUR 454.74)
**Orders:** 58, with an average order of EUR 50.32 and 134 units sold

I can't compare with July because the export only covers August. If you add July's export next time, every number will get a month-on-month comparison.

### What stands out

- **The second half of the month was 2.2× the first:** EUR 127.74 a day from Aug 16 on, against EUR 58.33 a day before that. The week of Aug 22–28 brought in EUR 1,128.70, which is 39% of the month.
- **Gift Set (EUR 833) and Glass Teapot (EUR 646) made 53% of product sales.** Black Tea sold the most units (27) but brought in only 11% of product sales.
- **Refunds came to EUR 70.50:** #1042 was refunded in full (EUR 60.50) and #1030 in part (EUR 10). The WELCOME10 code was used on 6 orders and cost EUR 20.28 in total.
